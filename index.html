Legal

Privacy policy

BEMS Pepper Pot respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

When you place an order we collect only what we need to prepare and deliver it: your name, phone number, delivery location and any note you add.

How it is used

Your details are used to confirm your order, deliver your food and contact you if there is a problem. Your order is sent to us through WhatsApp, so WhatsApp's own privacy terms also apply to that message.

What stays on your device

Your cart is stored in your browser only (local storage). It is not sent anywhere until you choose to order. Clearing your browser data clears your cart.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or share your details with third parties.
  • We do not require accounts, passwords or card details on this website.
  • We do not store payment information.

Contact

For any privacy question, message us on WhatsApp and we will respond.

Legal

Terms & ordering information

How ordering works

Add items to your cart, fill the short checkout form, then send the generated message on WhatsApp. An order is only confirmed after we reply to you.

Prices

All prices are in Nigerian Naira (₦) and shown on the menu. Small Chops prices start from the listed amount and may vary with the fillings you request — we confirm the final amount on WhatsApp before preparing.

Delivery

We deliver around Oye Ekiti and the FUOYE area. Delivery fees depend on your location and are confirmed on WhatsApp. Pickup is available where offered.

Availability

Meals are prepared fresh, so some items may sell out. Items marked unavailable cannot be ordered until they return. The Friday Combo Box is a Friday special.

Payment

Payment is arranged directly with us on WhatsApp. This website does not process payments.

Cancellations

Please tell us as soon as possible if you need to cancel. Orders already being prepared may not be cancelled.
